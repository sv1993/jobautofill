Install: unzip, open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, "Load unpacked", pick this folder.
(If you installed v1, remove it first or click the reload icon.)

How it learns new fields
1. "Save & fill page" fills the standard fields. Any field it can't answer appears in an
   orange "New fields found" box - type the answer once and click "Save answers & refill".
2. Or fill an unfamiliar form by hand, then click "Learn from this page" to remember every
   extra answer you gave (dropdowns and Yes/No choices included).
3. On other portals, similar questions (e.g. "Total experience in years" vs "Years of experience")
   are matched fuzzily and filled automatically. Manage saved answers under "Saved custom answers".

It never fills passwords, OTPs, captchas, ID numbers, or consent/terms checkboxes.
Always review the form before you submit.
